# Research and technology

Research runs in parallel fields; the player assigns a limited number of research slots (3 to start, more from events and doctrine). Each tech has a historical year, and researching ahead of it costs more (like HOI4's ahead-of-time penalty).

| Field | Example unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Hulls | Larger hull sizes, all-big-gun battleship, fleet carrier |
| Propulsion | Triple expansion → turbines → geared turbines, oil firing |
| Armour | Harvey → Krupp cemented → all-or-nothing schemes |
| Guns | Larger calibres, better shells, faster reload |
| Fire control | Rangefinders, director firing, radar ranging |
| Torpedoes | Longer range, faster, oxygen torpedoes |
| Aviation | Seaplanes, carrier aircraft, dive and torpedo bombers |
| Anti-sub and anti-air | Depth charges, sonar, dual-purpose guns |
| Damage control | Better compartments, firefighting, counter-flooding |

Nations get **doctrines** too (e.g. "Big gun", "Cruiser warfare", "Carrier strike"), which give modest bonuses and shape what the AI builds.
