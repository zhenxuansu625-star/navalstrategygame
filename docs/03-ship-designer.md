# Ship designer

The designer works like HOI4's: pick a hull, fill its module slots, and the design's stats and production cost come from the modules you chose. Naval experience unlocks and upgrades designs.

## Hulls

Each ship class has hulls tied to research years, e.g. Destroyer I–IV, Light Cruiser I–IV, Heavy Cruiser I–IV, Battlecruiser I–III, Battleship I–IV, Super-heavy battleship, Carrier I–III, Submarine I–IV. A newer hull has more slots, more HP and better base stats.

## Slots

| Slot type | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed: fire control | Basic → Improved → Advanced, radar ranging | Boosts hit chance of all guns |
| Fixed: engine | Light / heavy cruiser engines, battleship engines | Sets speed; better engines cost more |
| Fixed: armour | Light cruiser belt, battleship belt and deck | Sets armour value; adds HP |
| Main armament (front, mid, rear) | Light, medium, heavy and super-heavy batteries | Each battery adds light or heavy attack and piercing |
| Secondary | Secondary batteries, dual-purpose guns | Light attack vs screens; DP guns add AA |
| Custom | Torpedo launchers, AA guns, depth charges, sonar, radar, mine-laying, extra armour, catapult and floatplane | Fit the ship to its role |
| Carrier deck | Hangar space, deck armour | Sets air wing size |

Each module adds stats (attack, piercing, armour, AA, detection, visibility, speed, reliability) and production cost plus resources (steel, chromium). The designer shows the final stats, production cost per dockyard, and a comparison to the previous design and to known enemy designs.

**Roles and templates:** designs get a role name, e.g. "Torpedo destroyer", "Convoy escort", "Fast battleship". The player can save templates for quick updates when new modules are researched.

## Naval experience (XP)

A national pool earned from battles, naval exercises and some events. You spend it to:

- create a new design or variant (small cost),
- unlock doctrine branches,
- reduce the cost of retooling a production line to a new design.

## Refits

An existing ship can be refitted to a newer design on the same hull. This happens in a dockyard production line at a fraction of new-build cost, and the ship is out of service while refitting.

**Fleet experience** is separate from naval XP: it belongs to each ship's crew (see 05-battle-system.md).
