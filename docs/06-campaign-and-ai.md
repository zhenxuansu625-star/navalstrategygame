# Campaign, wars and enemy AI

The campaign runs in monthly turns from 1890 until 1945 or a decisive defeat. Wars switch the map into a strategic mode where fleets move between sea zones.

## Strategic map

- The world is split into about 20–30 sea zones, each with bases, trade routes and a value to each nation.
- In war, the player assigns fleets to zones with a mission: patrol, blockade, raid trade, escort convoys, or seek decisive battle.
- When enemy forces meet in a zone, a battle triggers based on mission, detection and weather, from small destroyer clashes to full fleet actions.

**Winning a war** is about **war score**: sinking tonnage, blockading enemy trade and holding key zones. At a threshold, the enemy sues for peace and you gain prestige, coastal states, or bases. Losing costs the same.

## AI navies

- Each AI has a doctrine and an industrial strategy (rush dockyards or grow civilian industry first), and designs its own ships with the same rules as the player.
- It reacts to your fleet: if you build fast battlecruisers, rivals research better armour or build their own.
- In battle, AI fleets pick sensible aggressiveness and repair settings and focus on missions that fit their doctrine.
- Difficulty adjusts AI factory output and starting crew experience, not hidden cheats.
